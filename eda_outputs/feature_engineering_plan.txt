BANK MARKETING - FEATURE ENGINEERING PLAN
==================================================

Purpose:
Create meaningful derived variables that can help the machine learning model identify customer segments and marketing response patterns.

ENGINEERED FEATURES
------------------------------
1. age_group - Groups customers by age.
2. balance_group - Groups customers according to account balance.
3. campaign_group - Groups customers based on the number of contacts during the current campaign.
4. previous_contacted - Indicates whether the customer was contacted in a previous campaign.
5. pdays_group - Groups customers according to the time since the previous contact.
6. previous_contacts_group - Groups customers according to the number of previous contacts.
7. contact_month - Keeps the campaign month as a marketing-related categorical feature.

IMPORTANT MODELING NOTE
------------------------------
The duration variable was excluded during preprocessing because it is only known after the customer interaction and could cause data leakage in a pre-contact prediction model.

The engineered features will be evaluated during model development based on their predictive usefulness.
